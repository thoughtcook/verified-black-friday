## 💡 Myth-busting
- **Ahrefs doesn't do Black Friday.** Its help center says it never runs seasonal discounts; the only saving is annual billing (~17%). Any "Ahrefs BF deal" is likely a reseller or scam.
- **Mullvad doesn't do sales.** Its pricing page says flat €5/month since 2009 and "no €1/month rate or lifetime membership".
- **"Lifetime" accounts sold on Reddit/Telegram** for Warp, Superhuman, Linear etc. are grey-market resales — not deals. We don't list them.
- **Headline "/mo" VPN prices** are usually billed upfront for 2+ years and renew higher. We note renewal prices where vendors publish them.

## ✅ How we verify
1. Every deal must link to the **official vendor page** (pricing, BF landing or official coupon URL).
2. If only a news/deal site reports it, it's marked `report` or 🟠 Unverified until the vendor page matches.
3. Each row carries a **last checked** date. Rows older than one recheck cycle get rechecked or downgraded.
4. Numbers are copied, never estimated. Missing price = we write "price not verified".
5. Last year's numbers are always prefixed **"Last year's (2025)"**.
6. Zero affiliate or referral links — ever.
